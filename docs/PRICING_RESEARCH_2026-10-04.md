# Pricing research and published proposal schedule — 4 October 2026

Official public pages checked: [Edufy](https://edufy.com.bd/), [IskulAI](https://iskulai.com/), [Fedena](https://fedena.com/pricing-and-plans).

| Provider | Public price observed | Capacity / benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Edufy | Basic 2,000 BDT monthly for 0–300; Standard 2,500; Premium 4,000. One-time charges 2,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 respectively. | Student bands change the subscription; external add-ons cost extra. |
| IskulAI | Basic 5 BDT/student/month; annual packages advertise two months free. | Larger campus requirements use a custom quote. |
| Fedena | Standard USD999, Premium USD1,399, Ultimate USD1,699 billed yearly. | Unlimited users; onboarding, training and updates. |

These are reference observations, not currency conversions or endorsements. Dynamic selectors can display zero before a student band is selected; band labels were used for Edufy. Competitor tax rates are not copied into our terms.

Our existing configured monthly prices are retained: Basic 1,500 BDT / up to 300 active students / 1 branch; Standard 3,000 / 750 / 2 branches; Premium 5,000 / 1,500 / 5 branches. Quarterly prices are 4,500 / 9,000 / 15,000. Yearly prices are 15,000 / 30,000 / 50,000 (12 months for 10 monthly payments). Initial connection fee: discuss and agree before setup. Larger capacity, imports, training and external services are scoped separately. Published capacity is a commercial allowance for new proposals, not a destructive database row cap. Existing contracts and records are preserved. No automatic checkout or payment capture is introduced.

The shared catalog is config/plans.php; landing prices and payment_settings monthly defaults read it. Demo enquiries preserve the chosen billing period in requirements without changing the existing request schema. Both languages have matching pricing copy.
